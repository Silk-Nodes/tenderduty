Missed / jail at counts the blocks missed in the chain's signing window against the number that gets the validator jailed. Each chain sets the window and the minimum share of it that must be signed, so the jail count differs per chain. The bar fills as the allowance is used and shifts from green to red on the way. Uptime is the share of the window that was signed.
